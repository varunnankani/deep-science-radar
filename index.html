How the radar works

A scheduled run searches public signals every morning, adds or updates companies, and publishes the changes here.

Where companies come from

  • Government grants: BIRAC, DST NIDHI, IN-SPACe, National Quantum Mission.
  • Incubators: Venture Center, C-CAMP, IIT Madras, SINE IIT Bombay, IISc SID and others.
  • Funding news across Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory, BioSpectrum and similar.
  • Patents and research papers, where a spin-out is named.

What each profile holds

  • Latest round, lead investor, and the partner who led it where coverage names one.
  • A founder snapshot from public reporting.
  • A plain-English explanation of the technology.
  • Parallel companies abroad. These are the radar's own comparisons, not claims from the source.
  • What to judge the company on next: the milestones that would change the view.

Radar score

FactorWeight

Each factor is scored 1 to 5 from public reporting, then weighted to a score out of 100. It's a judgement call to sort the list, not a rating.

Mapping to the RDI scheme

  • The ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation fund lends long-term at low or nil interest to private R&D in strategic sectors.
  • It funds work at TRL 4 and above, through fund managers: TDB across sectors, BIRAC for biotech.
  • TRL on each profile is estimated from the company's stage, so eligibility is a first read.

Limits

  • Most companies surface through funding news, after an investor has already arrived.
  • Grant lists and incubator pages are often undated PDFs, so some signal dates are approximate.
  • Where coverage doesn't name founders or partners, the profile says so rather than guessing.
  • The radar does not judge whether the science works.
