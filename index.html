Mapping to the RDI scheme

  • The ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation fund lends long-term at low or nil interest to private R&D in sunrise and strategic sectors. It is mostly debt, not grants or equity.
  • It funds projects at TRL 4 and above. Earlier work goes to ANRF's research programmes.
  • Companies apply through second-level fund managers: TDB for all sunrise and strategic sectors, BIRAC for biotech and allied areas. More fund managers have been approved but not yet named publicly.
  • TRL here is estimated from the de-risking stage, so eligibility is a first read, not a determination.
  • Why it matters to an investor: cheap non-dilutive debt can carry a company further on less equity, which changes the right round size.

Where the radar struggles

  • Most leads come from funding news, which reports a company after an investor got there. The uncontested count is the honest measure of how early the radar really is.
  • Grant awardee lists (BIRAC BIG, NIDHI) are PDFs, published rarely and without company detail.
  • Incubator pages list names without dates or stage, so a new cohort is hard to tell from an old one.
  • Patents and papers almost never name a spin-out; linking them to a company needs a human.
  • News sites disagree on round labels, amounts and headquarters, and some only give a week or month, so some signal dates are approximate.
  • Founder and IP scores come from public reporting only. Where founders aren't named, the team score is capped and confidence marked low.
  • It cannot judge whether the science works. Those leads are flagged for technical diligence.

Glossary

TRL
Technology Readiness Level, a 1 to 9 scale from an idea on paper to a product in use.
Pre-seed / seed
The first outside money in a company, usually to build and test the product.
Pre-Series A
A bridge round before the first big institutional round.
Tape-out
Sending a finished chip design to the factory to be made.
Fabless
A chip company that designs chips but pays a foundry to make them.
Precision fermentation
Using reprogrammed microbes to brew a specific ingredient, like a protein.
CRISPR
A tool that finds and cuts an exact piece of DNA.
Electrolyser
A machine that uses electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen.
Long-duration storage
Batteries that hold energy for many hours or days.
In-orbit servicing
Refuelling, repairing or moving satellites while they are in space.
Non-dilutive capital
Money that doesn't cost founders ownership, like grants or loans.
TDB / BIRAC
Government bodies that manage RDI money: TDB across strategic sectors, BIRAC for biotech.
IN-SPACe
India's agency that authorises and supports private space companies.
